Privacy Policy

Covers the SOCDesk website (socdesk.io) and the SOCDesk browser extension · Effective 2026-08-10

SOCDesk is a free, public threat-intelligence lookup tool for security analysts. It has no user accounts and no login. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to the indicators you look up and what data the tool does and does not handle.

Short version: the only thing SOCDesk sends anywhere is the indicator you choose to look up. We don't require an account, we don't build a profile of you, and we don't sell data.

What you submit, and where it goes

When you look up an indicator (an IP address, domain, URL, file hash, CVE or email), SOCDesk sends that single indicator to its own backend endpoint at socdesk.io/api/enrich. That endpoint queries public reputation and vulnerability services on your behalf and returns their assessments:

  • AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal, GreyNoise, ipinfo, MalwareBazaar (abuse.ch), and urlscan.io — for IPs, hashes, domains and URLs;
  • CISA KEV, NVD and FIRST EPSS — for CVEs.

Those third-party services receive the indicator you looked up and handle it under their own privacy terms. SOCDesk passes the indicator as a query parameter and never fetches the indicator itself (an attacker-supplied URL never becomes an outbound request on your behalf). API keys used to query these services live only on the server and are never exposed to your browser.

Look-up requests are cached briefly at the edge to reduce load; the cache key is the indicator and type only, and nothing about you is stored with it.

What stays on your device

Anything you mark, save, or configure — extension settings, watchlists, review state — is stored locally in your own browser (local storage) and is never transmitted to SOCDesk. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

The browser extension

The SOCDesk extension only acts on indicators you explicitly choose: text you select and send via the right-click menu, or text you paste into its popup. It does not read the content of the pages you browse, does not run on pages in the background, and does not track your browsing. It talks only to the SOCDesk origin you configure (default socdesk.io). Its declared permissions are limited to the context menu, local settings storage, and network access to that one origin.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no cross-site tracking.
  • No collection of the pages you visit or your browsing history.

Aggregate analytics

The website may use privacy-preserving, cookieless aggregate analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand overall traffic volume and performance. This measures page views in aggregate; it does not use cookies, does not identify you, and does not track you across other sites. The browser extension uses no analytics.

Children

SOCDesk is a professional security tool and is not directed to children.
