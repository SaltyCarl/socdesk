Branding · palette study · SOCDESK

Three accents on the pixel system — seen next to the verdict tones

Same unified pixel identity in all three: the mug-as-O emblem (S · mug · C over a width-matched DESK), the topbar mark, and a live consensus-tally card. Only the accent changes. The card is the real test — the brand accent has to stay clearly distinct from the green / amber / red verdict tones it sits beside, because those are reserved for the tally and can't also be the brand.

What to look for: in each card, does the accent (the mug band, the active tab, the COPY button) read as a different thing from the verdict dots — green amber red — or does it blur into them? Teal sits closest to the green "clean" tone; steel-blue and coffee are furthest from all three.
Steel / Ice Blue#4F9AD6
Recommended · instrument

A desaturated instrument blue — reads like an aircraft / terminal panel, not a neon cyber dashboard. Clearly off the teal default, still cool and technical, and the furthest of the three from every verdict tone.
