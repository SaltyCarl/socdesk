The Muster

A row of source-ticks on a periwinkle scope-line — the analyst's paper tally, rendered as an instrument. Verdict sets direction & height (flagged rises above the line, benign is a stub below, no-data sits on the line); a diamond cap marks an authoritative catalog fact standing above the votes. It's a literal count — no arc, no percentage, no invented magnitude — and it reads before colour, so it survives colour-stripped email clients and colour-blindness. Verdict red/amber/green rides per-source ink only; the count and the diamond carry urgency, not a block wash.
