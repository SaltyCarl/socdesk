01 · Construction

The cup silhouette & the saucer

Three moves make it read as an elegant cup rather than a flowerpot or a mug: the rim is the widest point; the sides curve (a symmetric cubic wall, a whisper of flare at the lip, tucking inward) down to a narrow, rounded base; and that base seats into a saucer. The saucer is two stacked ellipses — a deeper-caramel underside peeking below a caramel top — with a hairline raised inner rim and a soft contact shadow where the cup's foot meets it. The two-tone is flat, not a gradient: one lighter face, one slightly deeper shadow face divided just right of centre. Clean enough to hold from 188px down to the 16px favicon (where the saucer is dropped).

04 · Fresh-eye read
What changed, and why it lands

  

    

      
Shape Cup + saucer

      
The handled mug becomes an elegant cup on a saucer. Rim widest, curved walls to a narrow rounded
        base, an elliptical saucer with a raised rim and a foot contact shadow. The saucer + foot + curve are what
        read as an elegant cup rather than a mug or a flowerpot.

    

    

      
Ceramic & steam Caramel + wavy

      
Restored to the locked warm caramel two-tone — a lighter #C68B5B face over a
        deeper #915B32 caramel shadow face and saucer underside, flat and clean, no gloss or
        bloom. Steam is settled on wavy: smooth periwinkle lines in
        #7C8AFF / #4A4FD0.

    

  

  

    
Rounded cup silhouette, widest at the rimApplied
      
A symmetric cubic wall with a whisper of lip flare curves inward to a narrow, rounded base — a cappuccino-cup
        read, height roughly equal to the rim width. Not a straight cone, not a barrel mug.

    
Cup seated into the saucer wellFixed
      
Two stacked ellipses give the plate its thickness (deeper-caramel underside under a caramel top); a hairline inner
        rim marks the well; the cup is dropped so its foot meets the plate — no daylight — with a soft contact shadow
        directly beneath it.

Steam settled: wavyDecided

        lockup, topbar and favicon. The pixel cluster is kept small, marked superseded, for the record only.
