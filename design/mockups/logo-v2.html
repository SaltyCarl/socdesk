Coffee-mug mark v2 · refined dimensional · SOCDESK

A ceramic mug of coffee — not one brown blob.

The shipping mark fills the whole vessel with --coffee (#A6612F), so ceramic, liquid and body are one brown and it muds out. The fix is tonal separation: warm the vessel to a lighter caramel ceramic, keep a dark coffee surface, a cream rim, and periwinkle pixel steam — four materials, four tones. Dimension is flat (hard-edged facets) with zero glow; the only pixel/data nod lives in the steam. Three executions — including a SOC-native terminal-face mug — in both themes, proofed to 16px.

01 · The tonal fix

Warm the vessel so ceramic ≠ coffee

Same geometry, three vessel tones. The muddy original fills the body with the coffee token; warming it to caramel opens a clear light-vs-dark gap between vessel and surface. Greige separates even harder but reads cold/clinical against a warm system. Coffee surface stays #A6612F — only the ceramic body warms.

Muddy original Reject
Body = #A6612F. Vessel and coffee collapse into one blob — the exact failure we're replacing.
Caramel #C68B5B Pick
Warm and human. Clear gap over the coffee surface; cohesive with the espresso/paper system. The reference tone.
Greige #C6B39A
Separates hardest (lightness and saturation), but the desaturated stoneware reads a touch cold beside the warm accent.
02 · Choose an execution

Clean-flat, shaded-dimension, or the analyst's mug

Same caramel anatomy in all three. The choice is how much dimension and whether the mug carries a subtle command prompt. All simplify to the same favicon cut. The owner selected B — refined for register in §03.

A · Clean Flat

Single-tone ceramic + coffee. Every facet one flat fill — calmest, most "system icon". Pixel-dot steam.

B · Shaded Dimension

One ceramic shadow facet + one coffee crescent. Hard-edged, flat — reads crafted and dimensional with no glossy render.

C · Analyst's MugRecommended

Shaded body + an engraved >_ prompt. SOC-native — "built for long shifts at the desk" — with zero cyber cliché. Subtle detail, not a shouting terminal.

03 · Register shift · illustrated → icon

From “an illustrated coffee mug” to “a coffee mug that happens to be a UI icon”

The owner selected B · Shaded Dimension, but it sat ~10–15% too polished — an illustration dropped into a flat, geometric, monospace console. This is a subtractive, regularizing pass on the register only: same caramel/coffee/cream tones, same pixel steam, flat/no-glow. What changed — the organic S-curve body → a straight tapered trapezoid; the soft curved shade sliver → one deliberate flat plane on a straight seam; the 3D perspective lip → two concentric flat ellipses (top-down icon read); the smooth bezier handle → a squared bracket; and the coffee crescent, handle inner-shade and gold crema ring were removed. Nothing was added. The mug now rhymes with its own pixel steam and the console's geometry. Mug is also +10% in lockup/topbar.
