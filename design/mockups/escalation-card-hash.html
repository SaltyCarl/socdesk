Why identity, not a tally. A hash in MalwareBazaar is a near-deterministic fact, not six sources voting — so the card asserts it (source-subject-first: "MalwareBazaar catalogs…"), makes the family the hero, and shows VT's engine ratio as a supporting fact rather than the headline. The first-seen timeline is the rich visual that replaces the map — honest (it's just dates) and instantly graspable ("this has been circulating 17 months"). For an unknown file (no catalog hit), the hero degrades gracefully: "No catalog lists this file — VirusTotal: 3 of 72 engines flag it," and the family panel becomes "Unclassified."
